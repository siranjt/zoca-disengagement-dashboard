Your 4 coaching signals

Coaching loops — the 4 signals

RED UNTOUCHED >7d

RED customers with no logged contact in 7 days. Most urgent.

STALE RED >14d

Customers stuck in RED for 14+ days. Tier hasn't moved.

NO-REACH STREAK (3+)

3+ consecutive no-reaches against the same customer. Channel change needed.

SNOOZE IGNORED

Snoozes that elapsed without a follow-up. Promised attention not delivered.

The 4 talking-point flavors

Talking points — rule-based

CELEBRATE

3 customers recovered from RED since your last 1:1 — Maven Studio, Bloom Bar, Velvet Studio.

CONSTRUCTIVE

8 RED customers untouched >7d. Trend worsening (was 5 last week).

WARNING

MRR at risk: $4,250. Top accounts: Hcg Fit ($1.1K), Maven Studio ($890), Skin & Tonic ($720).

ASK

Anything blocking on the top 2-3 RED accounts: Hcg Fit, Skin & Tonic?

Zoca · Manager's Guide · 202614 / 28
Always lead with Celebrate. Never start a 1:1 with the warning. People shut down when warmth arrives last.

Field-tested workflows

Common manager workflows

CUSTOMER X IS AT RISK

Pull up their detail page. Screenshot the timeline. Send to AM in Slack with one line of context.

ATHARV KEEPS GETTING NO-REACH ON HCG FIT

Pull up the action log. See the pattern: 4 calls, 4 VMs. Suggest changing channel — try email or SMS.

WINS TO SHARE IN STANDUP

Use the team's snapshot timeline to find RED → GREEN recoveries. Celebrate publicly. Cheap morale win.

QUARTERLY LEADERSHIP READOUT

MRR managed, MRR at risk, total RED, recovery rate. All on the hero. Screenshot and ship.
